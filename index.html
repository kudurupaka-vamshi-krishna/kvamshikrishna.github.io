Research Biography

I am an Institute Postdoctoral Researcher in Transportation Systems Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IITB). I completed my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IITR) within the Human FRST Lab, focusing on the behavioral assessment and situational awareness of distracted road users.

My core research passion lies at the intersection of human factors, active road safety, intelligent transportation systems (ITS), external Human-Machine Interfaces (eHMIs), and advanced driver/pedestrian assistance systems (ADAS/PCAS). I combine cutting-edge empirical methodologies—such as Virtual Reality (VR) simulators, wearable eye trackers, Markov modeling, survival analysis, and machine learning—to design and deploy real-world assistive technologies.

Core Research Interests

Human Factors & Active Road Safety

Pedestrian distraction, situational awareness, and surrogate safety measures (SSM).

ITS & Assistive Technologies

Pedestrian Collision Avoidance Systems (PCAS) & Haptic Alert Wearables (HAWS).

External HMIs (eHMIs) for Autonomous Vehicles

Real-time driver/pedestrian communication displays and cross-cultural evaluation.

VR & Eye-Tracking Experiments

Connected driving simulators, gaze fixation mapping, and spatial trajectory logging.
